📐 What is a Fraction?

A fraction represents a part of a whole. Think of it as dividing something into equal pieces and taking some of those pieces.

🔢 The Two Numbers

The numerator (top) tells you how many parts you have selected.

The denominator (bottom) tells you how many equal parts the whole is divided into.
